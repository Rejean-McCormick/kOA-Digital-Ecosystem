# Layer 04 — Structured Knowledge

**Layer status:** Conceptual layer  
**Normative for Kristal artifact contracts:** NO — Kristal v6 is normative.

## Function

This layer turns source-bound material into reusable, provenance-aware structured epistemic material. It explains the ecosystem role; it does not redefine Kristal schemas.

## Core question

What can be represented with enough provenance, uncertainty and structure to be reviewed, queried, referenced or reused without losing the meaning of the source?

## Kristal v6 mapping

Relevant artifacts/concepts include:

- Kristal State;
- optional Claim-IR / Resolved Claim-IR extraction profiles;
- assertion status and certainty;
- Working Exchange;
- Validation Report;
- Authority Recognition;
- Reference Exchange;
- Reader Policy;
- Runtime Pack.

The primary v6 boundary is **Kristal State**; it may carry reference knowledge, constraints, observations, rules, derived states, decisions and action candidates without imposing one domain ontology.

## Inputs

Sources, datasets, testimony, records, extracted claims, definitions, provenance, ambiguity, authority context and policy/mandate references.

## Outputs

Structured, provenance-preserving material suitable for deterministic compilation, review/validation, authority recognition, query and downstream use.

A Working Exchange may be produced before final validation/recognition where policy permits. Reference status is a separate authority-recognition outcome.

## Invariants

- preserve provenance, typed valuations and applicability;
- do not silently upgrade certainty or validation state;
- do not equate signature/integrity with epistemic validity;
- do not present Working material as Reference material;
- downstream readers obey their Reader Policy.
