# Multilingual functional chains and owner boundaries

**PROPOSED coordination view of documented owner interfaces.** Arrows are handoffs to inspect, not proof of a single integrated pipeline. Keep the owner locks unchanged.

| Producer → consumer | Contract or artifact | Boundary and negative gate |

| Phrase Ops → Wordbench | `semantik-wordbench-handoff-v1` | `release_authorized=false`, `release_eligible=false`; Wordbench reproduces evidence and owns linguistic review. |

| SA → Spek | typed request / deterministic `CommunicationResult`, declared language and RuntimeSet | No invention of facts, no unqualified text fallback; editorial assignment and external publication are separate. |

## Functional chain A — engineering

Lock exact RGL source → record language evidence → implement morphology/syntax/lexicon → compile and exercise scenarios → review exact candidate output → independent observation → owner release decision. Audit reports support investigation without changing state.

## Functional chain B — production

Use owner-approved semantic input → resolve reviewed lexical evidence → SA typed plan → qualified GF/PGF realization → explicit result/coverage/runtime identity → Spek composition and editorial review → separately authorized publication. Defluens orchestrates candidate work, Phrase Ops prepares candidates, Orchestrator handles release/activation and LevelUpDiag diagnoses. Do not imply any of these steps is automatically connected or currently operational.

## Required negative acceptance cases (not executed here)

- Reject mismatched hashes or unapproved schema/ACL; a bare KQ/KP or QID is not a namespace-bound identity.
- Reject silent locale substitution or unadvertised operation; a label does not establish semantic equivalence.
- Reject failed/missing mandatory scenario, unreviewed candidate, pilot-only evidence or unsigned/unapproved release where required by the owner.
- Reject invalid RuntimeSet identity and incompatible SA version; never promote by altering historical artifacts.
- Reject publication without an owner-approved editorial decision and target authorization; previews remain private.

Owner references are enumerated in [evidence.json](evidence.json). The acceptance gates belong in owner test suites; C0 records evidence, not results.
