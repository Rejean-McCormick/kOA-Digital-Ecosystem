# Hod — Architect Render (conceptual role)

Architect-Render produces human-facing representations from material allowed by the active Reader Policy.

## Rules

- preserve relevant artifact/assertion/certainty/validation/recognition labels;
- do not invent new factual claims;
- do not hide disputed/rejected/working status when material is shown;
- do not write directly into Kristal Exchange state;
- do not equate rendering with authority recognition.

The historical “validated inputs only” rule is replaced by explicit Reader Policy eligibility. Production surfaces may still choose `reference_only` or another strict policy.
